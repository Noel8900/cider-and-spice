STALL LICENSE AGREEMENT — CIDER & SPICE FOOD HALL LLC 1. PREMISES. Licensor hereby licenses to Licensee the exclusive use of Stall B4, approximately 120 sq ft, located at Cider & Spice Food Hall, Las Cruces, NM. 2. TERM. The license term commences August 1, 2026 and terminates July 31, 2027, unless earlier terminated in accordance with this Agreement. 3. LICENSE FEE. Licensee shall pay $2,400 per month, due on the 1st of each month. Late fees of 5% apply after a 5-day grace period. 4. REVENUE SHARE. A revenue share of 4% of gross monthly sales exceeding $8,000 shall be remitted by the 10th of the following month. 5. USE. Licensee shall use the Stall solely for the preparation and sale of food and beverage items consistent with the approved menu. No structural alterations without written consent. 6. HALL OS SYSTEM. Licensee agrees to use the Hall OS point-of-sale and order management system exclusively for all stall transactions during operating hours. 7. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days' written notice. Licensor may terminate immediately for cause including non-payment or health code violation.
