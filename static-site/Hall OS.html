System overview

Four operating modules. One shared infrastructure.

Each module addresses an operational requirement that an independent first-time food operator cannot resource on their own. Modules are independently scoped but share the same tenant population, the same commerce backend, and the same reporting layer.

01
Space OS

Physical infrastructure.

Commercial kitchen suite, walk-in refrigeration, dish pit, dining capacity, and the service flow that connects them.

  • 01.A

    ~$150K of kitchen capex, pre-built.

    Shared commercial kitchen, walk-in cooler, and dish pit eliminate the single largest capex line in a first-year restaurant operation.

  • 01.B

    Floor plan engineered for throughput.

    Stall fronts, runner paths, and seat-to-stall ratios are sized to service-period peak demand rather than nominal occupancy.

  • 01.C

    Centralized common services.

    Dining, restrooms, sanitation, and the bar program are operated by the Hub. Vendors operate only the line and stall front.

02
Brand OS

Brand & creative production.

In-house naming, identity, menu design, photography, and content scheduling — production-ready on or before opening day, licensed to the operator.

  • 02.A

    Identity & menu systems delivered pre-open.

    Each operator receives a verbal mark, visual identity, menu board, and signage system delivered prior to opening service rather than during the first quarter of operation.

  • 02.B

    Quarterly photography & content cadence.

    Stall photography and dish styling are produced on a quarterly cadence, supplying owned channels without requiring an in-stall marketing function.

  • 02.C

    Unified hall signage system.

    Wayfinding, stall facades, gift-card artwork, and promotional templates conform to a single specification, producing a coherent retail environment across all tenants.

03
Revenue OS

Shared commerce stack.

Unified POS, single inventory ledger, hall-wide loyalty, and scheduled multi-vendor remittance — every stall transacts against the same live system.

Online orders, counter tickets, and stall kitchen consumption write to a single inventory ledger. Stock-out propagates across every channel in the same transaction.

  • One POS terminal across every stall and the bar
  • Unified inventory across online & in-person channels
  • Real-time stock sync — prevents overselling
  • Auto stock-level adjustment on every sale
  • Live API updates push changes instantly
  • Restocking validates & updates in real time
  • Discrepancy flagging & auto error correction
  • Bulk inventory import via Excel for setup

Customer club, gift cards, cashback, and configurable promotions run natively in the commerce stack. No third-party integrations; one program scope spans every tenant.

  • Customer club for repeat-customer rewards
  • Gamification tools that reward return visits
  • Cashback program, native & hall-wide
  • Discount engine for configurable promotions
  • Gift cards — no third-party plugin required
  • Incentive programs for retention
  • Promotion functionality for offers & flash specials

Each stall is provisioned with a per-vendor dashboard; the operator receives a master console. Rent and fee deductions execute against the live sales feed on a scheduled remittance cycle.

  • Per-vendor panels — Amazon / Airbnb marketplace model
  • Individual stall dashboards for products & sales
  • Full order timeline: payment → fulfillment
  • Real-time sales feed for auto rent / fee deduction
  • Master operator panel across every stall
  • Financial reporting & vendor payout management
  • Multi-vendor marketplace functionality (Tier 3)
  • Vendor-separated reporting at every level
  • Multiple-domain support, one operating system
  • Automated tenant remittance — no manual math
04
Growth OS

Vendor scale-up & graduation.

Operational playbooks for staffing, systems, retail expansion, and standalone-location readiness.

  • 04.A

    Staffing & training playbooks.

    Role definitions, training SOPs, and food-safety documentation supporting growth from a two-person stall to a ten-person operation.

  • 04.B

    Defined graduation KPIs.

    Vendor-separated reporting — sales per stall, food cost percentage, customer rating, compliance status — produces the documented evidence required for standalone-location financing and lease negotiation.

  • 04.C

    Retail & CPG expansion support.

    Co-packing partnerships, label compliance review, and wholesale introductions to support migration of stall sidelines into retail SKUs.
