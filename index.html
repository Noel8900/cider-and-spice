The Opportunity

Las Cruces is ready. The niche is open.

A 300,000-strong regional market generates $178.7M in full-service restaurant receipts annually — yet residents drive 45+ minutes to Albuquerque and El Paso for the diversity they can't find at home. The Hub addresses three interconnected gaps simultaneously: dining variety, a craft cider destination, and a structured incubator for local food entrepreneurs.

The Concept

One roof. Every craving.

8,000 sq ft of indoor, climate-controlled space — designed for families, students, foodies, and visitors alike.

01

Permanent Anchor Stalls

7–8 stalls · 2+ year license

Dedicated private kitchens for established concepts. New Mexican & Mexican anchors, the Hub Signature Restaurant, Southern BBQ, Mediterranean, Asian Fusion — diversity is built in, not bolted on.

02

Rotating Incubator Stalls

2–3 stalls · 12–18 month cohorts

Emerging culinary entrepreneurs on a structured three-stage pathway from concept validation to full independence — with weekly coaching and marketing support.

03

Specialty Craft Cider Bar

20–25 rotating taps

8–10 New Mexico cideries featured alongside regional and international expressions. Cider Club membership, tasting flights, food pairings, and the social anchor for evening programming.

04

Ghost Kitchen Bays

2 delivery-only bays

$1,500–$2,000/month + 8% commission. Low-risk format experimentation for delivery platforms with full back-of-house support — no storefront overhead.

05

Shared Commissary Kitchen

$25–$35/hour rentals

Available for catering, wholesale production, and external food entrepreneurs. Modeled on The Kitchen Table Santa Fe, which launched with 15 pre-signed members and now serves 34 active users.

06

Common Dining Area

60–80 climate-controlled seats

Flexible for live music, trivia nights, Lotería, cultural festivals, and quarterly night markets attracting 300–500 regional attendees. Programming is core infrastructure, not an afterthought.

Vendor & Incubator Model

Your Launchpad to Independence

No structured culinary incubator exists in southern New Mexico. The Hub changes that — giving food entrepreneurs a lower-risk, mentor-supported pathway from concept to standalone restaurant.

Stage 1

Incubator Entry

Months 1–12

Milestone: Concept validated, menu refined, profitability established, 4.0+ Google rating.

Hub Support: Weekly coaching, NMED permit guidance, POS training, marketing integration, collective purchasing, insurance verification.

$2,000–$2,500/month + 8% of gross sales

Stage 2

Permanent Stall

Months 13–18

Milestone: Sustained profitability + loyalty proven → offered a permanent stall license within the Hub.

Hub Support: Long-term license, mentorship role for next cohort, ongoing operational and marketing support.

$2,800–$3,200/month + 6% of gross sales

Stage 3

Independence

Months 19–36

Milestone: Graduation to a fully independent brick-and-mortar location in the Las Cruces region.

Hub Support: SBA loan guidance, commercial site selection, business plan development, alumni network access, optional co-investment (5–15% equity stake).

Validated Model

Proof of Concept — Right Here in New Mexico

The Hub's model is proven. Two direct New Mexico comparables and a national growth trend confirm both the demand and the format.

Sawmill Market

Albuquerque, NM
  • 35,000–40,000 sq ft · 28–30 food concepts
  • Cited by name in the City of Las Cruces East Lohman Development Plan as a positive development precedent
  • Demonstrated that food halls build community affection — longer visits, higher spend, repeat customers
"People stay longer, come back more often, and spend more money in places that attract their affection." — Jim Long, Heritage Cos.

The Kitchen Table

Santa Fe, NM
  • Opened May 2023 in a smaller NM market than Las Cruces
  • Launched with 15 pre-signed commissary members → now serves 34 active members
  • Santa Fe Chamber Business of the Year (2023) & Edible Magazine Local Hero Award (2024)

Directly validates the Hub's commissary kitchen model and confirms demand from food entrepreneurs who need lower-barrier access to commercial kitchen space.

National Industry Trend

Food Hall Market, USA
  • Food halls grew 26% while traditional hospitality contracted (2025)
  • 25 food halls in 2010 → 360 by 2023 → 700+ projected by 2027
  • Las Cruces sits in a completely untapped gap within an actively growing national category

The national tailwind makes Las Cruces a timing opportunity — not an experiment. The format is validated; the market is open.
